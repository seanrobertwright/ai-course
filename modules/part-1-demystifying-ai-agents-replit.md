# Part 1: The New Era of Creation — Demystifying AI Agents & Replit

> **Target Duration:** 60–90 Minutes  
> **Audience Background:** Zero coding experience, zero AI agent experience.  
> **Primary Goal:** Overcome the fear of technology, understand the difference between chat and agents, and build a live app in under 20 minutes.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. Explain how software development changed from manual coding to autonomous AI agents.
2. Differentiate between an **LLM Chatbot** (adviser) and an **AI Agent** (doer/builder).
3. Set up and navigate the **Replit** workspace with zero local computer configuration.
4. Issue a first natural-language command to Replit Agent and observe the autonomous development loop in real time.

---

## 🖼️ Slide-by-Slide Presentation Guide

### Slide 1: Welcome to the Builder Era
- **Visual:** Split screen showing a person staring at green-and-black scary matrix code on the left vs. someone typing a natural sentence and watching a clean modern app appear on the right.
- **Key Bullets:**
  - Coding used to be about syntax, memorization, and frustration.
  - Today, coding is about **ideas, logic, and clear communication**.
  - If you can explain an idea clearly to another human, you can now build software.
- **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Welcome everyone! If you've ever felt that building software was a superpower reserved only for computer science graduates, I have good news for you: that era is officially over. Today, you don't need to learn how to write lines of syntax or memorize arcane commands. You are stepping into the role of an Architect. You describe what should exist, and autonomous AI agents will build it for you. By the end of this session, every single one of you will have a live, working application running on the internet."*

---

### Slide 2: The Evolution — From Calculators to Agents
- **Visual:** A 3-step timeline:
  1. *Traditional Coding:* Human writes every single semicolon. (Slow, high barrier).
  2. *AI Chatbots (ChatGPT/Claude):* Human asks questions, AI gives text or code snippets, human still has to paste and run it.
  3. *AI Agents (Replit Agent):* Human gives a goal; AI writes code, creates files, installs libraries, runs the app, tests it, and fixes its own mistakes.
- **Key Bullets:**
  - **Chatbot = The Advisor:** Answers questions, writes text, but cannot touch your computer.
  - **Agent = The Construction Crew:** Has tools, takes action, executes commands, fixes its own errors.
- **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Most people have used ChatGPT to draft an email or summarize a document. That's a Chatbot—it’s an advisor. But imagine hiring a carpenter. A chatbot tells you how a chair is built. An Agent actually picks up the hammer, cuts the wood, tests if the chair wobbles, fixes the legs, and presents you with a finished chair. That is what an AI Agent is."*

---

### Slide 5: The Golden Rule of Prompting an Agent
- **Visual:** A comparison card:
  - ❌ *Bad Prompt:* "Make me a cool website."
  - ✅ *Good Prompt:* "Build a daily team lunch decision spinner where users can add restaurant names, spin a colorful wheel, and see the winning pick with confetti."
- **Key Bullets:**
  - **Goal:** What does the app do?
  - **Audience:** Who is using it?
  - **Core Feature:** What is the 1 main action the user takes?
  - **Style:** Clean, modern, playful, or corporate?
- **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Agents are brilliant, but they cannot read your mind. If you tell an architect 'Build me a nice house', you might get a log cabin when you wanted a glass villa. The secret to being a great AI builder is writing a clear, unambiguous starting specification."*

### Slide 6: Live Demo & Walkthrough — Building "The Decision Wheel"
- **Visual:** Live screen-share slide banner: "Instructor Live Demo".
- **Instructor Action:**
  1. Open replit.com.
  2. Click **Create** -> **Replit Agent**.
  3. Enter the starter prompt:
     > *"Build a modern, friendly web app called 'Lunch Roulette'. Let users type names of restaurants into a list, click a big 'Spin' button to randomly pick one, play an animation, and celebrate the winning restaurant with confetti. Give it a clean, mobile-friendly design with light and dark mode."*
  4. Watch the agent plan, create files, and display the live preview.
  5. Test it live on screen!

- **Visual:** Step-by-step student mission card.

  4. Click the preview URL on your mobile phone!
