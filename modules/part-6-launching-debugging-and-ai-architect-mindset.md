# Part 6: Launching, Debugging & The AI Architect Mindset

> **Target Duration:** 60–90 Minutes  
> **Audience Background:** Zero coding experience, completed Parts 1–5.  
> **Primary Goal:** Learn how to calmly diagnose and fix software bugs with the agent, deploy apps to production with custom domains, and adopt the long-term mindset of an AI Software Architect.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. Demystify errors: Understand why bugs happen and learn how to use Replit Agent as an expert troubleshooter.
2. Master the **3-Step Triage Technique** for fixing unexpected application behavior.
3. Deploy an application to production using Replit's one-click global hosting and attach a custom domain.
4. Transition from "non-coder" to an **AI Software Architect** capable of ideating, directing, and launching digital products at will.

### Slide 2: The Truth About Bugs (Engineers Break Things Too)
- **Visual:** A humorous comic or graphic showing that even 20-year veteran software engineers Google error messages every single day.
- **Key Bullets:**
  - Red text and error popups are **not** failures; they are clues.
  - Software is not fragile glass; it's digital clay.
  - Replit Agent loves error messages—they provide exact instructions on what to fix.
- **Speaker Script:**
  > *"When beginners see red text in a console or an app crashes, panic sets in: 'Did I delete the internet? Did I break my computer?' The answer is absolutely not. Even senior staff engineers at Google and Apple deal with errors every single hour. In fact, an error message is the best thing that can happen because it tells the agent precisely which file, line, and character needs adjustment."*

---

### Slide 3: The 3-Step Agent Debugging Triage
- **Visual:** A 3-step triage checklist:
  1. **Copy the Error:** Grab the red error message from the Replit console or preview banner.
  2. **Feed It to the Agent:** *"I got this error when clicking 'Submit': [Paste error]. Please analyze the cause and fix it."*
  3. **Visual Screenshot Debugging:** Take a screenshot of the broken UI, upload it into Replit Agent, and circle the issue.
- **Key Bullets:**
  - Let the agent read its own logs.
  - Use visual screenshots when layout or alignment is off.
  - If a prompt ruins everything: use Replit's **Checkpoints (History)** to roll back 10 minutes with one click.
- **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Here is your foolproof debugging protocol. Step 1: Copy the error text. Step 2: Feed it back to the agent without panic. Step 3: If it's a visual glitch, take a quick screenshot, drop it into the chat, and say: 'The card on the right is overlapping the navbar; fix the spacing.' And if the agent gets confused, hit the checkpoint rewind button and start fresh."*

### Slide 4: Development vs. Production (Hosting Made Simple)
- **Visual:** Diagram comparing:
  - **Development (Your Workspace):** The workshop/kitchen where you test and see live changes. Can go to sleep when you close the laptop.
  - **Production (Global Deployment):** The permanent storefront on high-speed cloud servers running 24/7/365 across the world.
- **Key Bullets:**
  - In development, your app is for you to test.
  - When you hit **Deploy**, Replit packages your app into a high-availability cloud server.
  - Your users get lightning-fast loading speeds and 99.9% uptime.
- **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Right now, your app runs in your Replit workspace—your personal digital workbench. But what if 10,000 people visit your site at once? Or what if you close your laptop to go to sleep? You want your app to stay online 24/7. That's what 'Deployment' does. Replit takes your app, puts it on permanent cloud servers, and keeps it alive forever."*
